Why I built it, and how it works

The problem I lived with

In HR, labour law questions are answered from memory and hearsay. The Act and Rules are long, and nobody has time to search them for every query.

How it works

AskBLA answers from the text of the Labour Act 2006 and the Rules, so each answer points to the section it came from. An AI reviewer checks the answers every hour and flags weak ones for me to fix.

What's next

Extending it beyond the Labour Act to other rules HR teams work with, including banking-sector HR instructions.

Why I built it, and how it works

The problem I lived with

An exit interview held on someone's last day, across a desk, rarely gets the real reason. And the notes end up scattered, so patterns never reach leadership.

How it works

OUTSYT is triggered as part of the clearance process, so every exit gets a proper conversation instead of an occasional formality on the last day. The employee has a private chat that adapts to their answers, in English or Bangla. HR sees the full picture, line managers see only aggregates, and the consent note explains anonymity up front.

Where it stands

A working prototype in private testing, with HR leaders giving feedback. Part of a wider plan to reimagine the employee lifecycle, starting with onboarding.
